Intake form

Finding out more about you so we may be better prepared and customise our movement assessments to the individual.

About you

The basics, so Sotir knows who he is training and how to reach you.

Health screen

Answer honestly. Nothing here rules you out, it tells Sotir how to start you safely.

Has your doctor ever said that you have a heart condition and that you should only perform physical activity recommended by a doctor?*
Do you feel pain in your chest when you perform physical activity?*
In the past month, have you had chest pain when you were not performing any physical activity?*
Do you lose your balance because of dizziness, or do you ever lose consciousness?*
Injury and medical history

This shapes your movement assessment more than anything else on the form.

Have you ever sprained your ankle?*
Have you ever had any head injuries?*
Have you been knocked out in the past 6 months?*
If you have any medical exams (x ray, CT scan, MRI or ultrasound) please upload them
Lifestyle

How you spend the other 23 hours decides what your training has to undo.

Does your current occupation require extended periods of sitting?
Does your occupation require you to wear shoes with a raised heel (dress shoes)?
Rate your sleep quality

Goals

The most important part of the form. Be specific.

A copy of your completed form is emailed to you as a PDF the moment you submit. Your answers go straight to Sotir and are used only to prepare your movement assessment. Nothing is shared with anyone else.
