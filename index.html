The Trouble · Pomorie to Melbourne
The coach

Forged across three continents

Sotir Kichukov grew up in Pomorie, a fishing and salt town on Bulgaria's Black Sea coast that the ancient world knew as Anchialo. He first stepped onto the mats in 2006, became a Bulgarian kickboxing champion at only his second competition, then turned professional and reigned through the Max Fight promotion to claim its bantamweight championship.

Around 2015 he signed with ONE Championship and became recognised as the first Bulgarian to compete in the promotion, sharing the cage at flyweight with names such as Yusup Saadulaev, Mark Striegl and Danny Kingad. He coached at Saigon Sports Club in Vietnam before settling in Melbourne, where he now leads Combat and Performance Training.

When the gyms closed, the work did not

The training

Built to make you complete

Striking and grappling trained together, never as rivals. Sotir coaches the whole fighter, the mechanics, the mindset and the recovery, so you get sharper every session.

01

K1 Kickboxing & Boxing

Sharp, relaxed stand up under K1 rules. Kicks, knees, hands and clinch, with stance, angles, timing and output built from the ground up.

02

Wrestling & Grappling

Clinch, takedowns, control and a deep submission game that has finished fights at the top level.

03

Fight IQ & Movement

Read the round, move with purpose and make the smart decisions that win exchanges.

04

Prehab & Conditioning

Build a body that holds up. Corrective work, strength and cardio for real world durability.

05

Nutrition & Recovery

Fuel, sleep and mineral balance. How you recover shapes what you can do under pressure.

06

Mindset & Readiness

Always be prepared. Resilience trained as a skill, so you show up ready when it matters.
