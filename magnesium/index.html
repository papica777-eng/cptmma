Why recovery matters

Everyone talks about the work you do in the gym. Almost nobody talks about the work your body does after. Magnesium sits at the centre of that quiet recovery.

Muscle

Function & cramp

Magnesium plays a role in normal muscle function. Training hard depletes it, and topping it up supports the muscles that carry you through camp.

Nervous system

Calm & reset

After a hard round your system is switched on. Magnesium contributes to a normal nervous system, which helps you settle and reset between sessions.

Sleep

Deeper rest

Sleep is where you actually adapt. Better mineral balance supports the deeper, more restful sleep that turns training into progress.
